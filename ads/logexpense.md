# LogExpense – product brief for ads

Source: github.com/aramboi4/expense-tracker-saas (landing, About, Plans and Try-the-App pages).
Only claims listed here may be used in ads. Update this file when the product changes.

**Website:** logexpense.site (say it as "log expense dot site")
**One-liner:** Track money together, not alone.
**What it is:** A shared expense, loan, and budget tracker for families, roommates, and small teams who want one place to track money together.

## Who it's for
- Families tracking household spending, groceries, rent, shared subscriptions
- Roommates splitting rent, bills, and groceries
- Small teams tracking shared expenses
- Anyone paying off a loan (including a family loan) who wants reminders and a payment history

## Features (all available today)
- **Shared workspaces** with Admin and Contributor roles; invite people by email. Everyone in a workspace sees the same numbers.
- **Loan tracking** with automatic due-date reminders and a full payment history. Loan status (Not Started, Active, Fully Paid) updates itself from payments.
- **Smart budgets** that auto-populate from your accounts and loans, so there's nothing to rebuild every month.
- **Summary Dashboard**: balances, subscriptions, budget usage, and loans at a glance.
- **Monthly statements** of your workspace.
- **Notifications** for due payments and budget thresholds, in-app and optionally by email.
- **Privacy:** never connects to real bank or e-wallet accounts; everything is entered manually. Ledger and account names are encrypted in the database.
- **Web app** (works in any browser) and a **free Android app** (download from the website; not on the Play Store yet).

## Plans
- **Free:** 1 admin + 2 contributors per workspace, up to 3 custom account types, 3 custom transaction types, 1 active loan.
- **Plus** and **Pro:** more members (Plus: 2 admins + 5 contributors; Pro: 3 admins + 15 contributors) and unlimited custom types and loans. Paid checkout is not live yet (coming soon), so ads should push the Free plan.

## Not available yet (never claim these)
- iOS app (coming soon)
- Paid checkout (GCash, Maya, Atome, cards: coming soon)
- SMS alerts (planned)
- Bank syncing (by design, it never links to banks)
- "Instant" or "real-time" balances: expense effects on balances apply after a 1-day edit window, so
  never say instant/real-time/live updates. "Everyone sees the same numbers" is fine.

## Tone
Friendly, relatable, practical. Show a real everyday money problem (who paid the electric bill? did we pay the loan this month? where did the grocery money go?) and the app as the simple fix. No made-up statistics, reviews, or user counts.
